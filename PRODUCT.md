# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers booking aesthetic or skin care clinic appointments on phones and desktops, and a clinic administrator managing availability and reviewing customer bookings.

## Product Purpose

Offer a three-step journey: choose a clinic, choose an available appointment, enter contact details. Share live availability and bookings with a protected admin panel. Deploy React, Netlify Functions, and managed Postgres within free-plan allowances for a small demo.

## Brand Personality

Calm, refined, professional. The user selected the calm studio direction with ivory, olive, and spacious layouts, and requested the name Clinical Appointment Demo.

## Anti-references

The user explicitly rejects an AI-generated appearance. Avoid noisy landing-page patterns, excessive decoration, generic feature grids, flashy gradients, and animations that delay booking.

## Design Principles

Make the next decision obvious. Reveal complexity progressively. Keep appointment details visible as they accumulate. Give the administrator direct control without clutter. Make demo status and real booking outcomes unambiguous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive labels, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support as proposed in the design preference question.
